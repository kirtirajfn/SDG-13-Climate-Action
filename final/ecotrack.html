HIGH IMPACT
🚲 Switch to cycling or walking
For trips under 5km, replacing your car with cycling or walking eliminates transport emissions entirely and improves personal health.
💚 Saves up to 2.4 kg CO₂/day
HIGH IMPACT
🌱 Adopt a plant-based diet
Replacing one beef meal per day with a plant-based option dramatically reduces your food footprint. Beef produces 20x more emissions than vegetables per 100g protein.
💚 Saves up to 3.3 kg CO₂/day
MEDIUM IMPACT
☀️ Switch to renewable energy
If your utility allows it, switching to a green energy tariff can cut your home electricity emissions by up to 88%. Solar panels offer long-term savings too.
💚 Saves up to 1.8 kg CO₂/day
MEDIUM IMPACT
✈️ Reduce flights
One return flight from KL to London produces about 1.5 tonnes of CO₂. Opting for trains or virtual meetings for short-haul trips makes a big difference.
💚 Saves up to 1.5 tonnes per trip
MEDIUM IMPACT
💧 Reduce hot water use
Shorter showers and cold washing cycles reduce energy needed for heating. A 5-minute shower uses 35L less water than a 10-minute one.
💚 Saves up to 0.6 kg CO₂/day
LOW IMPACT
📦 Buy less, buy better
Fast fashion and frequent online purchases create hidden emissions from manufacturing and delivery. Choosing secondhand or durable goods reduces consumption footprint.
💚 Saves up to 0.5 kg CO₂/purchase
